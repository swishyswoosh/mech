Mech is an independent Bluetooth controller for the Sphero R2-D2 droid.
It works offline, without an account or an Internet connection.

Use the landscape cockpit to drive with a joystick, turn the dome, select
a stance and play favorite sounds. Browse the droid's built-in sounds and
animations, adjust its lights, or run a repeating rainbow effect.

Draw and run paths, save lighting presets and create sequences of actions.
The cockpit shows live sensor readings, and the Depot provides diagnostics
and optional battery profiling. Light and dark themes are available.

Switching away from Mech stops active control while retaining the Bluetooth
connection. Disconnect puts the droid to sleep.

Requires a Sphero R2-D2 droid and Android 8 or newer with Bluetooth Low Energy.
Android 8–11 require location permission for Bluetooth discovery; Mech does
not use location data. Android 12 and newer use nearby-device permissions.
